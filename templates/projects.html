RISE Lab · Simulation research

Coordinating two robotic arms around a shared object

Challenge: Coordinate two opposing UR5e arms and RG2 grippers while preserving a stable shared grasp.

My role: Built and refined the Isaac Sim setup, corrected joint frames, added collision checks, and implemented object-space goals with per-arm inverse kinematics.

Outcome: In one 26-second simulation run, all measured samples retained four-finger contact, with no unexpected collisions and 0.135 mm maximum slip. These are simulation results; physical calibration remains in progress.

NAU undergraduate capstone · UGRADS

Shot difficulty and scoring across the NBA and NHL

How much of a player's shooting efficiency reflects the difficulty of the shots they take? This capstone uses matched 2014–2024 NBA and NHL shot data to study expected scoring, shot difficulty, and player performance.

Open full-resolution graph to zoom

My work: Developed data preparation and validation workflows, shot-quality models, a Shot Difficulty Index, and visual comparisons of player performance. The team presented the capstone at NAU UGRADS.
